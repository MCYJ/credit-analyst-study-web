Before you begin

Is this an official KBI app?

No. RushLabs provides an independent learning tool. It does not register candidates, award qualifications or provide official pass decisions.

Are Android Pro and the iOS app the same product?

Android Pro is a separate paid product in preparation; iOS uses an update to the existing app. Purchases or subscriptions do not automatically transfer between these products or platforms.

Does practice match the official test format?

The included content and shown app screens are Korean. App choice counts, question counts and scores can differ from the official five-choice examination. Practice results are not official pass decisions.

Does it provide credit ratings or investment advice?

No. It is for personal exam study, not actual company credit ratings, lending eligibility, investment decisions or financial advice.
